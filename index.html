How an engagement works

  1. Assess. A fast, hard look at the operation, the numbers and the team.
  2. Stabilise. Fix what's costing money now. Put basic control and KPIs in place.
  3. Build the team. Strengthen or recruit the management bench, and install the rhythm they'll run to.
  4. Drive the step-change. People, process, systems and KPIs, delivered to an agreed plan.
  5. Hand over. A team that owns the results, with no ongoing dependency on ProcessWorks.

Engagements typically run three to 18 months, scoped and priced up front.
